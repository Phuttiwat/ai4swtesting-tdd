# Clarifying questions (Activity 1)

Group labels: **[Do now]** / **[Later]** / **[Drop]**

## Accuracy

1. **[Do now]** How should time that isn't a multiple of 30 minutes be recorded?
   Time is tracked in half-hour units, but what happens to 20 or 45 minutes? Rounded up/down/nearest, or rejected?
2. **[Do now]** What are the minimum and maximum amounts of time that can be entered?
   Is 0 allowed? 0.5 the minimum? A daily cap like 24h? Negative values for corrections?
3. **[Do now]** What does "a project" mean, and where do valid projects come from?
   Predefined list or free text? "Proj A" vs "proj a" could become two projects.
4. **[Drop]** When is the time recorded against: a specific date, a week, or a start/end time?
   We only record *how much* time, not *when*.
5. **[Do now]** How is "how much time I have spent" calculated and shown in the history?
   Running total per project, per-day breakdown, or list of entries? Decimal hours or h/m?

## Consistency

6. **[Later]** What does "complete a timesheet" mean compared with saving an entry?
   Is a timesheet one entry or a collection? Is "complete" = submit/save/lock?
7. **[Do now]** Can one timesheet hold time for several projects, and can the same project appear twice?
   If the same project is submitted more than once, should the hours be merged (added) or kept separate? (used in Activity 4)
8. **[Later]** Who exactly is "I", and does "only I can see my tracked time" exclude managers/admins?
   Only one user for now; access control matters later.
9. **[Later]** Can tracked time be edited or deleted after it is entered, and by whom?
   History consistency / audit trail.
10. **[Drop]** Why is the half-hour unit required, and does it apply everywhere (timers, imports, reports)?
    No timers/imports/reports in scope.
